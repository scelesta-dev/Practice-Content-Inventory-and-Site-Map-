# Site Map Template: 
Polytechnic Community garden Website

## Site Purpose: 
This website will provide information on garden hours, volunteer opportunities, workshops, and accessibility. The goal is to boost community engagement and make information easier to access. 


## Primary Audience: 
The audience is students, faculty, staff, and nearby residents. 


## Primary User Tasks

1. Find garden hours
2. Learn about workshops
3. Explore volunteer opportunities
4. View accessibility information
5. Contact garden organizers

## Proposed Site Map

- Home
  - Purpose: Introduce the community garden to visitors and share its mission and activities. 
  - Key content: Garden introduction, mission statement, operating hours, garden photos
- Volunteer Days
  - Purpose: Inform visitors about volunteer opportunities and educational workshops on gardening. 
  - Key content: Volunteer schedules, participation details, workshop topics, and upcoming activities
- Visit and Accessibility
  - Purpose: Help visitors plan their visit and provide location details, gardening hours, and accessibility information. 
  - Key content: Garden location, operating hours, parking
